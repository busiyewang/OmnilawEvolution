# Recorded discovery feed

`manifest.json` replays two real arXiv API searches made on 2026-10-25 ("language-agnostic sentence
embedding", "paraphrase identification") plus the phase-1 sources for "news deduplication".
The `arxiv_*_abstract.txt` files contain only titles and abstracts as returned by the arXiv API,
quoted for research use; full texts are not stored. Copyright remains with the authors.
